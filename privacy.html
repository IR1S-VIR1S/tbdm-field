TBDM Field privacy policy

TBDM Field is the data-collection app and Google Sheets workbook used by the research team of the TB-DM study. It is not a public service. Only people invited by the principal investigator can use it.

What is collected

Study data entered by the research team, as approved by the study's ethics committee. The Google sign-in permissions requested by the workbook's script are used only to run the study workbook: reading and writing its spreadsheet, sending invitation and reminder emails, and updating the workbook's own script.

Where it is kept

Entries are stored encrypted on the team member's phone until they are sent to the study workbook in the principal investigator's Google account. Data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or sent to any other service.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this study's data go to the principal investigator of the TB-DM study.
